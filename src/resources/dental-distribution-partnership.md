MEIJIA DENTAL welcomes inquiries from dental distributors that serve clinics and laboratories in their local markets. Our production base in Shenzhen Bao'an supports fixed and implant restorations, removable prosthetics and orthodontic appliances. A first conversation helps both sides identify the product lines and workflow that fit your customers.

## Tell us what your market needs

In your first message, include your country or region, the types of customers you support and the products they ask for most often. For example, you may receive regular inquiries for zirconia crowns, cast partial frameworks or clear retainers. An approximate case volume and the typical type of records you receive help our team understand the opportunity.

## Agree on how cases will move

Discuss who gathers the prescription and scans or models, how your team will communicate case changes and who will handle questions after delivery. Share the documentation your customers expect and any product or delivery requirements you need us to review. Pricing, delivery and commercial terms can be discussed once the scope is clear.

To get started, [contact MEIJIA DENTAL](/#contact) with the subject **Distribution partnership** and the region you serve. You can also [review our product lines](/products) before sending your inquiry.
