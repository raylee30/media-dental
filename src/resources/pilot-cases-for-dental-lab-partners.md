A first case shows how two laboratories work together in practice. MEIJIA DENTAL invites partner laboratories to discuss a limited pilot, with a representative case and a shared understanding of what both teams will review.

## Choose a case that represents your usual work

A routine product from your normal case mix gives both sides a concrete reference. Include the product, current prescription, available scans or models, shade reference and target delivery date. Tell us which stages you want to review before fabrication and which details can be handled under your usual instructions.

## Decide what you will compare

- **Intake.** Did the team identify missing records and agree on the scope before design?
- **Review.** Were questions about fit, contour, shade or components easy to resolve?
- **Delivery.** Did both teams understand the expected date, packing and handover?
- **Feedback.** Can changes for the next case be recorded in a way that both teams can use?

Share that feedback after the pilot. It gives both teams a starting point for discussing recurring cases, communication and delivery arrangements.
