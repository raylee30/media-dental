MEIJIA DENTAL offers two product lines for fixed restorations. Our all-ceramic line includes zirconia and lithium disilicate options. Our porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) line uses a metal framework with a porcelain outer layer.

## All-ceramic restorations

Our [all-ceramic product line](/all-ceramic-restorations) covers zirconia crowns, lithium disilicate veneers, inlays and onlays. When requesting a quotation, tell us the restoration type, your material preference and any shade or translucency requirements.

## Porcelain-fused-to-metal restorations

Our [PFM product line](/porcelain-fused-to-metal) includes single crowns and multi-unit bridges. Share the unit count, shade and any metal preference so our team can review the case with you.

## What to share with your inquiry

- The restoration type and number of units
- Available digital scans or impressions, bite records and shade references
- The expected delivery date and any clinic-specific instructions
