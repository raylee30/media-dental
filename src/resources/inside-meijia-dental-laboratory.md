MEIJIA DENTAL originated in Hong Kong and established production in Shenzhen in 2005. Our Bao'an laboratory serves dental clinics, distributors and partner laboratories.

## From case intake to finishing

We review incoming case files and prescriptions before moving to digital design, material processing and technician finishing. Our production covers fixed restorations, implant restorations, removable prosthetics and orthodontic appliances.

## Final inspection and dispatch

Before dispatch, our team reviews the finished work against the case prescription, including fit, contacts, occlusion, shade and surface finish. Cases are then documented and packed for delivery.
